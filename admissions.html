Admissions 2026/2027

Everything you need to know before you apply.

Age criteria

Class Minimum age

Age is counted as on 1 April of the admission year.

Eligibility requirements

  • Child meets the minimum age for the class
  • Entry assessment and interview for Grade 1 and above
  • Previous school report card and result
  • School leaving certificate for transfer students
  • Parent or guardian meeting with admissions office

Required documents

  • Copy of child’s B-Form or birth certificate
  • Copy of father’s and mother’s CNIC
  • Four recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school leaving certificate and report card
  • Immunisation card (Playgroup to Prep)

Fee plans and policies

Level Admission Monthly
  • Monthly fee is due by the 10th of each month
  • 10% sibling discount from the second child
  • Merit scholarships for top performers
  • One month’s notice required for withdrawal
